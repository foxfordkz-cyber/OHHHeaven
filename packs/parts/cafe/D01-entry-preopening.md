## Bulk sale (NY)
- SX tax.ny.gov TB-ST-70 https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/st/bulk_sales.htm : purchaser must file AU-196.10 at least 10 days before paying or taking possession; Dept replies within 5 business days with AU-197.1 (release) or AU-196.2 (Notice of Claim); within 90 days of receipt Dept must tell purchaser actual amount; purchaser liable up to greater of sales price or FMV; if AU-196.2 received -> put full purchase price in escrow; buyer also owes sales tax on tangible personal property bought (furniture, fixtures, supplies; except inventory for resale) - pay to seller w/ final return or directly to Dept. Seller remains liable.
- form: https://www.tax.ny.gov/pdf/current_forms/st/au196_10.pdf ; registered mail (secondary sources)
- CPA Journal 2016: https://www.cpajournal.com/2016/03/16/new-york-state-bulk-sale-notification-requirement/ ; totalfood: https://totalfood.com/buying-a-restaurant-watch-out-for-the-sellers-sales-tax-liabilities/
