## Winter at the door
- DSNY snow laws: clear within 4 h if snow stops 7am-5pm, 14 h if 5pm-9pm, by 11am if 9pm-7am; path >= 4 ft; corner — path to crosswalk. https://www.nyc.gov/site/dsny/what-we-do/snow-response/snow-laws.page ; https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-01397
- Fines: DSNY $100 / $150 / $250 (1st/2nd/3rd+); penalty schedule 16 RCNY 19-103: 100-350 / 150-350 / 250-350. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCrules/0-0-0-99532
- Admin Code 16-123(b): ice frozen too hard — ashes, sand, sawdust; remove as soon as weather permits. https://law.justia.com/newyork/codes/2006/new-york-city-administrative-code-new/adc016-123_16-123.html
- Admin Code 7-210: abutting owner liable, nondelegable; tenant liable if created the condition / special use / by lease indemnity. https://www.newyorkappellatedigest.com/2019/10/24/...
- Storm-in-progress doctrine: no liability during storm + reasonable time; 5 h after — covered; 12 h after + freeze — not covered. https://www.newyorkappellatedigest.com/2017/07/19/storm-in-progress-rule-did-not-apply-storm-stopped-12-hours-before-the-slip-and-fall-2nd-dept/
- The Hartford 2025: slip/fall/customer injury avg claim $45,000 (2020-2024), 20% of claims. https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2025/12/10/850545.htm
- DSNY Feb 27 2026 testimony: >4,500 summonses after Winter Storm Fern (Jan 25 2026). https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dsny/downloads/about/news/testimony/2026/dsny-oversight-hearing-testimony-022726.pdf
- Central Park normals 1991-2020 snowfall: Dec 4.9, Jan 8.8, Feb 10.1, Mar 5.0 in; snowy days >=0.1in: Dec 2.1, Jan 3.7, Feb 3.2, Mar 2.0.
- Avero, storm Stella: ~half of restaurants closed, open ones -19% vs same day last year. https://averoinc.com/avero-index-snowstorm-stella-and-restaurant-sales/
- Fiserv Jan 2026: limited-service restaurants sales -3.4% YoY, traffic -5.0%. https://www.fiserv.com/en/insights/small-business-index-resources/winter-storm-fern-fiserv-special-report-january-2026.html

## Months before opening
- Free rent for build-out 3-6 months typical, up to 12 for heavy work; free rent often base rent only. https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/build-out-provisions-in-nyc-restaurant-1727571/
- DOB stop work order violation: first offense $6,000. https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/property-or-business-owner/stop-work-order.page
- NYS Senate 2017 "Cooking without gas": 17% got gas service on time; avg >$140,000 lost per business. https://nysenate.gov/sites/default/files/press-release/attachment/cooking_without_gas.pdf
- NBAT: opened 75 days earlier (33% faster), 2014. https://www.nyc.gov/office-of-the-mayor/news/327-14/
- CRT: rent < $250k exempt; full credit < $500k and income <= $5M. https://www.nyc.gov/assets/finance/downloads/pdf/25pdf/2025-crt-report.pdf
- Good guy guaranty: 3-6 months notice; liable until surrender. https://www.metro-manhattan.com/good-guy-guaranty/

## Buying an operating business
- BizBuySell coffee shops & cafes: median sale $150,000; median revenue $375,000; SDE $78,780; earnings multiple median 2.00 (1.50-2.55), revenue multiple 0.41 (0.30-0.57); 2025 median sale $176,500, sale/ask 0.90. https://www.bizbuysell.com/learning-center/valuation-benchmarks/coffee-shops-cafes/
- NY Tax Law 1141(c): AU-196.10 >= 10 days before paying/possession, else buyer liable for seller's sales tax. https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/st/bulk_sales.htm
- FSE permit not transferable; new owner applies; $280. https://nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/description/food-service-establishment-permit
- Key money listings: $150k East Village 2nd-gen, $160k Harlem cafe. https://commercialobserver.com/2013/08/weighing-in-on-key-money-deals/

## Floor area and crowding
- Occupant load: 15 net sq ft/person tables & chairs; 7 chairs only; 5 standing (IBC 1004.5 / NYC BC 1004.1.3).
- Cafe seat density 12-15 sq ft per seat typical, 10-12 tight QSR, 20-25 premium. https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/average-restaurant-square-footage
- Barista throughput: one barista ~60 drinks/h on 2-group. https://www.seattlecoffeegear.com/blogs/scg-blog/drinks-per-hour
- Queue: fast-casual max wait 5 min (34%), 10 min (30%) — Waitwhile 2023 via White Hutchinson. https://whitehutchinson.com/news/agrinews/2024/january/article102.shtml
- ED field data: each extra person visible in queue +0.5 pp abandonment (Batt & Terwiesch). https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/losing-patience-and-patients-what-makes-people-wait-in-line-or-decide-to-bail/
- Knoeferle et al. 2017 J. Retailing: social density inverted-U on spending (40,000 baskets). https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jouret/v93y2017i4p541-549.html
